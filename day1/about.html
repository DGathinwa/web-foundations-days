About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightning-fast application designed to help you capture your thoughts instantly. It lives in your browser and ensures you never lose a great idea again.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the application in your browser.
  2. Type your thought into the text input box.
  3. Press the "Add Note" button or hit Enter to save it.

Features

  • Instant loading times
  • Keyboard accessible
  • No cloud sync required (local storage)

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Save the current note
